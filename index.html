The story

A plan years in the making.

Years ago, Masamune Makabe was a shy, chubby kid who was harshly rejected by Aki Adagaki, the girl he liked.

He never forgot it. He got fit, worked on himself and came back to town as a completely different person, with a plan to make her fall for him and then turn her down just as coldly as she once did.

But revenge is rarely simple. New people, old feelings and a few surprises keep pulling his plan off course.

Characters

The people in the plan.

The main group of friends and rivals, kept spoiler-free.

M

Masamune Makabe

The lead. Once teased and rejected, now determined and polished, with a revenge plan he's sure will work.

A

Aki Adagaki

The girl at the center of the plan. Admired by everyone at school, and not as simple as her reputation suggests.

Y

Yoshino Koiwai

Aki's loyal maid and attendant, with a lot more going on behind the scenes than it first seems.

N

Neko Fujinomiya

A gentle, affectionate transfer student from a wealthy family who quickly becomes a rival for Masamune's attention.

K

Kojuro Shuri

Masamune's best friend in class, often mistaken for a girl because of his petite, delicate looks.

T

Tae Futaba

The energetic class representative, and a supportive friend to the whole group.
